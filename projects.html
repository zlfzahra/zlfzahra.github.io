Final Project · Aug 2023 – Jul 2024

The Design and Development of a Food Waste Recycler Machine Prototype

A household-scale food waste recycler machine designed to process organic household waste into ready-to-use compost fertilizer. The machine integrates mechanical, electrical, and informatics domains using the VDI 2206 design methodology.

The system incorporates sensors to monitor key composting parameters, including carbon, nitrogen, oxygen, moisture content, pH, and temperature, with consideration of SNI 19-7030-2004 compost standards. The final design resulted in a physical prototype, 3D CAD models, technical drawings, and system design documentation.

Institution: Politeknik Manufaktur Negeri Bandung Dimensions: 350 × 450 × 450 mm
