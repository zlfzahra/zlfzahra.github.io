Projects

Food Waste Recycler Machine

An engineering design project focused on developing a household-scale food waste recycler machine prototype to process organic waste into raw material for compost fertilizer.

Cutter Machine

A paper cutter machine designed for accurately cutting stacks of paper using a mechanical cutting mechanism, with emphasis on machine structure and assembly design.

Automatic Stirrup Bending Machine

A mechanical system designed to automate the bending of steel rods into stirrups for reinforced concrete structures, improving speed, accuracy, and consistency.

Flat Conveyor

A belt conveyor system designed for horizontal material transportation in manufacturing, logistics, and packaging applications, enabling continuous movement of materials with minimal manual intervention.

PICCO - Transmission

A compact mechanical power transmission system designed for small-scale applications, focusing on efficient power transfer and the relationship between torque and rotational speed.

Material Handling Device

A mechanical system designed to transport, store, and control materials within production environments while reducing manual handling and improving operational efficiency.

V-Shaped Roller Conveyor

A specialized conveyor system with V-shaped rollers designed to guide and stabilize cylindrical objects such as pipes and rods during material transportation.

Waste Washer Machine

A mechanical system designed to clean and separate contaminants from recyclable materials using washing, centrifugal, and material separation mechanisms.
